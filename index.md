# Possum Run: Play Dead! Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

Possum Run: Play Dead! ("the app") is a game by Wise Owl Tech ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your data.

## What we collect
**We do not collect, store or receive any personal data.** There are no accounts, no analytics and no servers operated by us. Your score and progress exist only on your device while you play and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app removes the app and its on-device data.

## Fonts (Google Fonts)
The app's display fonts (Baloo 2 and Nunito) are loaded from Google Fonts when your device is online. That request goes directly from your device to Google and, like any web request, can include your IP address and device information as part of the request. We do not receive, store or have access to any of that data. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The game also works offline, using fallback fonts.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Data sharing and security
We do not sell or share data, because we hold none. The only network traffic the app generates is the font loading described above, which uses encrypted connections.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the date above will change.
